MeCollage Pro AI Features: Smart Face Centering and Local Background Removal

Published: January 29, 2026 · Updated: November 5, 2026 by MeCollage Team

MeCollage's AI features are designed to remove repetitive editing work, not to replace your judgement. Two tools are available in the editor: Smart Face Centering and Local Background Removal. Both run in your browser, so your photos do not need to be uploaded to a server.

This article explains what each feature does, how to use it, when it works best, and where its limits are.

How the AI features work

Both tools use browser-based AI models. The first time you use an AI feature, your browser downloads the model, which can take a little longer on a slow connection. After that, processing happens on your device using WebAssembly and, where available, GPU acceleration.

Your photos are read locally by the browser. MeCollage does not store them, and the AI models run against the image data in your browser session.

Smart Face Centering

When a photo is cropped into a collage cell, faces near the edge can be cut off. Smart Face Centering detects faces in each photo and adjusts the image position inside its cell so the largest detected face sits closer to the centre.

How to use it

  1. Add your photos to the collage.
  2. Open the AI panel with the 🤖 button in the bottom toolbar.
  3. Click Apply to All Photos.
  4. Wait while each photo is analysed. A confirmation appears when faces are detected and centred; if no face is found, you will be told so.

When it works best

  • Portraits, group photos and event photos where the subject is clearly visible.
  • Collages with many people photos at 4:5, 1:1 or 3:4.
  • Photos where the face is large enough to detect reliably.

Limitations

  • Very small faces, heavy occlusion, extreme angles or stylised illustrations may not be detected.
  • Detection is automatic; if a result doesn't look right, adjust the crop manually by choosing a different photo position or aspect ratio.
  • The feature optimises framing inside the existing cell; it does not change the grid itself.

Local Background Removal

Background removal creates a transparent cut-out of the main subject. It is useful for product collages, profile visuals, seasonal cards and clean editorial layouts. Unlike cloud-based background removers, the processing happens locally in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Select the photo you want to edit on the canvas.
  2. Open the AI panel if it is not already open.
  3. Click Remove Background and wait for processing to finish.
  4. The photo is replaced by a transparent-background version in the same cell. Re-export after you are happy with the result.

Getting the best cut-outs

  • Start with a clear subject against a reasonably simple background.
  • Good lighting and contrast between subject and background help the model.
  • Complex hair, transparent objects and very busy backgrounds are the hardest cases and may need a second attempt.

Limitations and performance

  • The first run downloads the model; later runs are usually faster.
  • Very large images take longer to process. If your browser feels slow, try a smaller source image.
  • The feature works best in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari versions; older browsers may not support the required APIs.

Combining both features

A practical workflow for people-led collages is: remove distracting backgrounds first, then apply face centering, choose the final aspect ratio, and finish with a filter, text and stickers. Export at the end so all AI edits are included in the final PNG.

Privacy and cost

The AI features are included in MeCollage at no cost and do not require an account. Because processing is local, the main cost is the one-time model download and your device's processing power. Photos are not sent to MeCollage servers.

Which feature should you use first?

If your main problem is framing, start with Smart Face Centering: it changes only the photo position inside its cell and is easy to evaluate at a glance. If your main problem is a distracting background, use Background Removal first so the cut-out is final before you arrange the grid. For a portrait collage, the most reliable order is background removal, face centering, aspect ratio, then text and export.

Frequently asked questions

Do the AI features upload my photos?

No. The image data is processed in your browser. The models themselves are downloaded from a CDN the first time you use the feature.

Why did face centering not detect a face?

The detector may miss faces that are very small, turned away, covered or stylised. You can still position the photo manually inside its cell.

Can I undo AI background removal?

Background removal replaces the selected photo in the editor. If you need the original, re-add the photo from your device. Keeping a copy of the original photo is always a good habit.
