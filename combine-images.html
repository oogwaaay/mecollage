How to combine images in 4 steps

1. Add your pictures

Drag files onto the canvas, paste a screenshot with Ctrl + V, or use the upload button.

2. Select the ones to merge

New photos are selected for you. Use Select all, Shift-click, or drag a box to pick several.

3. Pick a layout

Choose side by side, stack or grid, then set the gap, border, rounded corners and background.

4. Download or copy

Export one PNG, JPG or WebP file, or copy the merged image to the clipboard.

Frequently asked questions

How do I combine two images into one?

Add both photos to the canvas, select them, and choose Side by side or Stack. The preview shows the exact output size; then export one PNG, JPG or WebP file.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Photos are opened, arranged and exported by your browser. They are never uploaded to MeCollage.

How many images can I combine at once?

Up to 20 photos in one collage. For a longer strip, combine them in batches and then combine the results.

Can I make one long image from chat screenshots?

Yes. Add the screenshots in order and choose Stack; widths are matched so the strip has straight edges. Drag a screenshot to reorder it.

Which image formats can I use?

JPG, PNG, WebP and most formats your browser can display. Export as PNG, JPG or WebP.

Is MeCollage free?

Yes. Combining, cropping, filters, text and export are free, with no signup and no watermark.
