Step-by-Step Tutorial

1

Choose Your Aspect Ratio

Select the format that fits your needs:

  • 1:1 Square - Perfect for Instagram posts and profile pictures
  • 9:16 Vertical - Ideal for Instagram Stories, TikTok, and mobile wallpapers
  • 16:9 Landscape - Great for YouTube banners and presentations
  • 4:5 Portrait - Optimized for Instagram vertical posts
2

Upload Your Photos

Add up to 20 photos from your device. Simply:

  • Click the upload area to browse files
  • Or drag and drop photos directly onto the page
  • Supported formats: PNG, JPG, WEBP
  • No file size limits
3

Arrange Your Layout

Your photos will be automatically arranged, but you can customize:

  • Gutter Spacing - Adjust space between photos (0-50px)
  • Corner Radius - Add rounded corners for a softer look
  • Photo Order - Drag to rearrange images
4

Add Creative Touches

Personalize your collage with:

  • Photo Filters - Noir, Retro, Fade, Glow, Cool effects
  • Text - Add captions, quotes, or messages
  • Emoji Stickers - Decorate with ✨❤️🔥🎭 and more
5

Export Your Collage

Download your finished collage:

  • Choose PNG or JPG format
  • High-quality export, no watermark
  • Ready to share on social media or print

💡 Pro Tips for Better Collages

  • Use photos with similar lighting or color schemes for a cohesive look
  • Arrange photos chronologically to tell a story
  • Keep it simple - don't overload with too many photos
  • Use filters consistently across all photos for harmony
  • Leave some breathing room with gutter spacing
  • Add subtle rounded corners for a modern look
  • For Instagram, use 1:1 for posts and 9:16 for Stories

How to Make a Photo Collage on iPhone

On iPhone, open MeCollage in Safari or Chrome — no app download is needed. Tap the upload area, choose photos from your library (up to 20), select an aspect ratio such as 1:1 or 9:16, and arrange the grid. Export the finished collage as PNG, then save it to Photos or Files from the share sheet.

Tip: if a photo is not in the right shape, crop a single image to 1:1 first with the square crop tool and then add it to the collage.

How to Make a Photo Collage on Android

On Android, open MeCollage in Chrome. Tap the upload area and pick images from Gallery or Google Photos, then choose a ratio and drag photos into the order you want. Use the gutter and corner controls for spacing, add text or stickers if needed, and download the PNG to your Downloads folder.

How to Make a Photo Collage on Windows

On Windows, open MeCollage in Edge, Chrome or Firefox. Drag photos from File Explorer into the upload area, or click to browse. Arrange the layout, switch between 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 3:4, 4:3 and 16:9, then export the collage and open it from your Downloads folder.

How to Make a Photo Collage on Mac

On Mac, open MeCollage in Safari, Chrome or Firefox. Drag images from Finder or the Photos app into the editor, choose the ratio that matches where you will share the result, and export a high-quality PNG. Everything runs locally in the browser, so your photos are not uploaded to a server.

Which device and layout should you use?

The editor works the same on all four platforms; the main difference is how you pick files. For a square Instagram post, choose 1:1. For a phone wallpaper or Story, choose 9:16. For a presentation or YouTube banner, choose 16:9. If you need a different arrangement, switch the grid layout to Custom and set rows and columns manually.

Turn a single photo into a square, or build a square collage

If your goal is one photo cropped to a clean 1:1 frame before you build the collage, use the square crop tool. If you want to arrange several photos into a square composition, open the square collage maker.

Not sure which shape to build? The aspect ratio calculator converts any width or height into 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 or 21:9 pixel sizes before you start a new collage.
