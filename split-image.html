Frequently asked questions

How do I split an image into 9 parts?

Choose the 3×3 layout, add your image and press Download ZIP. You get nine PNG tiles named tile-1.png to tile-9.png.

Which split layouts are available?

2 across, 2 down, 2×2 and 3×3. The grid preview shows exactly where the cuts will be.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image is opened in your browser, cut with canvas and packed into a ZIP locally.

What format are the tiles?

PNG, one file per tile, packed into a single ZIP. Transparency is preserved.

Does splitting reduce image quality?

No. Each tile keeps the source pixels at full resolution; only the edges are cropped to tile boundaries.

Is the splitter free?

Yes. Free, no signup and no watermark.
