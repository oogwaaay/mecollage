Blur creates complex pixel gradients that don't compress as efficiently as solid colors or sharp edges. A photo with heavy blur borders will have a larger file size than the same photo with a solid color border or a cropped version. If you need smaller files for email attachments or platforms with upload limits, try Color fill mode (solid colors compress best) or reduce the JPG quality setting.
