What Is an Aspect Ratio?

An aspect ratio is the width of an image divided by its height. It is written as two numbers separated by a colon, such as 16:9 or 4:5, and it describes the shape of the image rather than its pixel count. A 1920×1080 photo, a 1280×720 video and a 3840×2160 display all share the same 16:9 shape.

The first number is the width and the second number is the height. 16:9 means that for every 16 units of width there are 9 units of height. That is why a 1920×1080 image is 16:9: 1920 ÷ 120 = 16 and 1080 ÷ 120 = 9. The same logic turns 1080×1350 into 4:5 and 1080×1920 into 9:16.

An aspect ratio does not tell you how large an image is. A 640×360 image and a 3840×2160 image are both 16:9; one is simply higher resolution. When you resize an image, keep the ratio the same to avoid stretching faces, circles and text.

Instagram Aspect Ratios and Pixel Sizes

Instagram feed images must sit between 1.91:1 and 4:5, which is why the same photo can look very different depending on how it is cropped. The square 1080×1080 post is the classic format, but 1080×1350 (4:5) uses more vertical space and is the portrait size most feeds show first. 1080×1920 (9:16) is the size for Stories and Reels, while a 16:9 landscape image is displayed at about 1080×608 in the feed.

For the full platform-by-platform chart, see the social media image sizes page. If you need an exact 1:1 crop, use Square Crop to zoom, pan and choose the part of the photo that stays. If you want the whole photo visible with a blurred or coloured background instead of cropping, Square Image is the better fit. For a 9:16 Story built from several photos, Instagram Story Collage lays the images out in a vertical canvas.

TikTok Sizes: Why 9:16 Wins

TikTok's video player is vertical, so 1080×1920 (9:16) is the native size. A 1:1 image works as a profile photo at 200×200, and a 4:5 or 16:9 video will be cropped or letterboxed. Design at 1080×1920 and keep captions away from the top and bottom edges, where the app's interface sits. If you are adapting a horizontal clip, crop a vertical section rather than shrinking the whole frame into the middle of the screen.

YouTube Sizes: 16:9 Videos, 9:16 Shorts

YouTube's standard video and thumbnail shape is 16:9: 1920×1080 for the video and 1280×720 for the thumbnail. Shorts are vertical at 1080×1920 (9:16). YouTube also uses 1:1 images for channel avatars and podcast covers, and vertical videos can receive an automatically generated 4:5 thumbnail in some feeds. If you are making a thumbnail, keep the text inside the safe area and check how it looks at small sizes.

Website Image Sizes: Hero, Cards and Ultrawide

Websites are flexible, but a few shapes cover most needs. A 16:9 hero at 1920×1080 is the standard wide banner, and 1280×720 is a lighter alternative. A 4:5 card at 1080×1350 works for product grids and portrait features, while 9:16 at 1080×1920 fits mobile hero sections and story embeds. A 21:9 ultrawide banner usually uses 2560×1080 on a 64:27 display, which is the practical shape sold as "21:9".

How to Crop or Resize an Image to an Aspect Ratio

  1. Find the ratio you need in the calculator. A 4:5 Instagram post, for example, uses 1080×1350.
  2. Crop the photo to that shape so faces and text are not stretched. Square Crop handles 1:1; any editor can crop to the other ratios at the pixel size shown.
  3. If you are combining several photos, Square Collage keeps every cell at 1:1, and Rounded Corners softens the result for cards and social posts.
A useful rule: change the crop first, then resize. Cropping after resizing throws away pixels you already processed, and resizing without cropping is what makes portraits look stretched.

What About 21:9? Strict Ratio vs Display Industry

Strict 21:9 is 2.333:1. At a height of 1080 pixels the exact width is 2520×1080. Most monitors and TVs sold as "21:9" are actually 64:27 (2.370:1), such as 2560×1080, and 3440×1440 is 43:18 (2.389:1). That is why this calculator uses 2560×1080 as the practical 21:9 preset and shows the strict 2520×1080 value in this explanation. For social media, the ultrawide shape is not native anywhere — keep a 16:9 or 9:16 version for posting and use 21:9 on your own website or in video edits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an aspect ratio?

An aspect ratio is the width of an image divided by its height, written as two numbers such as 16:9. A 1920×1080 image has a ratio of 16:9 because 1920 ÷ 1080 = 1.78, and 16 ÷ 9 = 1.78.

How do I calculate the aspect ratio of an image?

Divide the width by the height. For example, 1080 ÷ 1350 = 0.8, which is 4:5. To simplify a size to a ratio, divide both numbers by their greatest common divisor: 1920 and 1080 both divide by 120, giving 16:9.

What is my aspect ratio if I only know the width and height?

Enter the width and height in the calculator. It divides the width by the height, reduces the result to the simplest ratio (for example 1920×1080 becomes 16:9) and also shows the closest common ratio from 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 and 21:9.

What is the closest common aspect ratio?

The calculator compares your width-to-height value with five standard ratios — 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 and 21:9 — and shows the nearest one. For example, 1200×628 is 1.91:1, whose closest common ratio is 16:9.

What size is 16:9 in pixels?

Common 16:9 sizes are 1920×1080 (Full HD), 1280×720 (HD), 2560×1440 (QHD) and 3840×2160 (4K). YouTube videos and thumbnails and most website hero images use 16:9.

What size is 4:5 on Instagram?

Instagram's portrait feed post is 1080×1350 pixels (4:5). It gives you more vertical space than a square post while still fitting the feed.

What size is 9:16 for TikTok and Reels?

TikTok videos, Instagram Reels and Stories, and YouTube Shorts all use 1080×1920 pixels (9:16).

Can I post a 16:9 image on Instagram?

Yes, but it appears as a landscape post around 1080×608 pixels. Feed images must sit between 1.91:1 and 4:5, so 16:9 fits, though 4:5 uses more of the screen.

What is 21:9 in pixels?

Strict 21:9 is 2.333:1, so at 1080 pixels high the width is 2520 pixels. Most displays sold as 21:9 are actually 64:27 (2.370:1), such as 2560×1080, and 3440×1440 is 43:18. The calculator uses 2560×1080 as the practical 21:9 preset and explains the difference.

No. The aspect ratio calculator only does arithmetic in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere, and it works without an account.
