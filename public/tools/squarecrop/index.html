Frequently Asked Questions

What is Square Crop 1:1?

Square Crop 1:1 is a free online tool that crops your images to a perfect square (1:1 aspect ratio). Perfect for profile pictures, Instagram posts, and any platform that requires square images.

How does it work?

Upload your image and use the zoom slider to adjust the crop area. Drag the image to reposition it within the square frame. The tool ensures your image is cropped to a perfect 1:1 ratio while giving you full control over what to keep in frame.

Should I crop or use Square Fill?

Use Square Crop when you want to focus on a specific part of the photo (great for portraits and avatars). Use Square Fill when you want to keep the entire photo visible with blur or color backgrounds added. Both are free and work instantly in your browser.

Is Square Crop free to use?

Yes. Square Crop is completely free, with no watermarks, signup requirements, or hidden fees. You can crop unlimited images and download them at full quality.

Does it work without signing up?

Yes. No account or email is required. Open the tool, upload an image, and download the result. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Which file formats can I download?

You can download your cropped image as JPEG, PNG or WEBP, with a custom output size if needed. Your image is processed locally and never uploaded to a server.

When should I crop instead of using blur/color fill?

Crop when the background is distracting or irrelevant, when you are making a profile picture or avatar, or when the photo has a clear subject you want to focus on. Use Fill with blur or color when it is a group photo where everyone matters, a landscape with important context, or when you do not want to lose any part of the image. A simple test: if cropping away the edges feels like you are losing an important part of the story, use Fill instead. Crop mode also produces the smallest file size because it removes pixels rather than adding background.

What pixel size do I need for a 6×6 inch photo print?

Print shops need 300 DPI (dots per inch). 6×6 inches × 300 = 1800×1800px minimum.

Our default 1200px is only good for 4×4 inch prints. For 8×8 inch or larger: Use Custom size and set 2400px.

Most phone cameras output 3000-4000px, so cropping to 2400px won't lose quality. Common mistake: Using 1200px for large prints — the result looks soft and blurry when printed.
