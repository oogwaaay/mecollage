How to Make a Birthday Collage

1. Gather Your Photos

Select the best birthday photos - portraits, party shots, cake moments, and group pictures. We support up to 20 images.

2. Choose Your Layout

Pick a square format (1:1) for Instagram, or vertical (9:16) for stories. Adjust the grid to fit your photo count.

3. Add Birthday Touches

Customize with birthday-themed stickers (🎂🎉🎈), add "Happy Birthday [Name]" text, and apply festive filters.

4. Export & Share

Download your birthday collage in high quality and share it on social media, send it to the birthday person, or print it as a gift.
