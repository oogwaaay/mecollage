Frequently asked questions

How is a freeform collage different from a grid?

A grid locks photos into equal cells. Here each photo keeps its own position, size and stacking order, so you can overlap them or leave deliberate white space.

Can photos overlap each other?

Yes. Click a photo to bring it to the front, drag it anywhere and resize it from the corner handle.

Can I split one photo into pieces?

Yes. Select a photo and use Split 2×2 or 3×3; it is replaced by tiles you can move apart, which is handy for Instagram grids.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos are decoded, arranged and exported in your browser; the board stays on your device.

Which formats can I export?

PNG with transparency, JPG or WebP, at 2× the board size; you can also copy the result to the clipboard as PNG.
