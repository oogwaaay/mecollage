Instagram post size

An Instagram portrait post is 1080×1350 (4:5); a square post is 1080×1080 (1:1), and the widest native feed landscape is 1080×566 (1.91:1). A 16:9 image still fits the feed but displays around 1080×608. Post at 1080 pixels wide and let Instagram handle the rest.

Instagram story size

Instagram Stories and Reels are 1080×1920 (9:16). Keep text and stickers away from the top and bottom edges where the app interface sits, and export at 1080×1920 so the Story never gets upscaled.

TikTok video size

TikTok is vertical-first: 1080×1920 (9:16) is the native video size. A 1:1 image works as a profile photo at 200×200, but a 16:9 or 4:5 video gets cropped or letterboxed. Compose inside 1080×1920 and keep captions in the safe zone.

YouTube thumbnail size

A YouTube thumbnail is 1280×720 (16:9). YouTube accepts larger 16:9 images (up to 3840×2160 for best quality), but 1280×720 keeps uploads small and is the size most creators design against. Keep text large because the thumbnail is often shown at a fraction of that width.

YouTube video size

YouTube video is 1920×1080 (16:9); Shorts are 1080×1920 (9:16) and the channel banner is 2560×1440 (16:9). The channel avatar is a 1:1 image at 800×800. Uploading 1920×1080 is enough for most videos — higher resolutions only help if the source footage is genuinely sharp.

Facebook cover photo size

A Facebook Page cover photo is 851×315 on desktop and 640×360 on mobile. The Page profile photo is 1:1 at 320×320, and Facebook Stories use 1080×1920 (9:16). The cover is cropped differently across devices, so keep the important part near the centre.

LinkedIn banner size

A LinkedIn profile banner is 1584×396 (4:1). LinkedIn post images are commonly 1200×627 (1.91:1), the profile photo is 400×400, and a company logo is 300×300. A 4:1 banner leaves room for a line of text but little height, so avoid stacking more than one message.

X header size

The X profile header is 1500×500 (3:1); post images are usually 1600×900 (16:9) and the profile photo is 400×400. The header is cropped on mobile, so keep the centre 1500×500 area readable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size is an Instagram post?

An Instagram portrait post is 1080×1350 pixels (4:5). A square post is 1080×1080 (1:1), and a landscape post is 1080×566 (1.91:1).

What size is an Instagram Story?

Instagram Stories and Reels use 1080×1920 pixels (9:16).

What size is a TikTok video?

TikTok videos are 1080×1920 pixels (9:16). A landscape 16:9 video is not native to TikTok.

What size is a YouTube thumbnail?

A YouTube thumbnail is 1280×720 pixels (16:9). YouTube also accepts larger 16:9 images and recommends up to 3840×2160 for maximum quality.

What size is a Facebook cover photo?

A Facebook Page cover photo is 851×315 pixels on desktop (640×360 on mobile).

What size is a LinkedIn banner?

A LinkedIn profile banner is 1584×396 pixels (4:1). LinkedIn post images are commonly 1200×627 (1.91:1).
