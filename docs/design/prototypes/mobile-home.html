Frequently asked questions

What is MeCollage?

A free browser-based collage maker with grid, gutter, radius, filters, text and stickers. Everything is processed on your device.

How many images can I add?

Up to 20 photos per collage. JPG, PNG and WEBP are supported.

What aspect ratios are supported?

1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 3:4, 4:3 and 16:9 — plus a custom grid up to 10 × 10.

How do I export my collage?

Tap Export in the bottom toolbar, pick PNG or JPG, and the file downloads directly. No account needed.

Is MeCollage free?

Yes — free, no watermarks, no sign-up. Photos never leave your browser.
